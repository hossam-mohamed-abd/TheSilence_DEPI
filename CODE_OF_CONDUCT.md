# Code of Conduct

MediSearch contributors are expected to maintain a professional, inclusive, and respectful environment.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Focus reviews on code, documentation, architecture, and product outcomes.
- Welcome questions and provide context for decisions.
- Respect privacy, security, and healthcare sensitivity in all project discussions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or secrets.
- Deliberately undermining project security, availability, or data quality.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this code of conduct.
